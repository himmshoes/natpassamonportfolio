Mission Statement

Our core goal was to declutter the user flow and maximize service discovery, establishing a unified interface that supports users’ everyday financial actions without overwhelming them with options.

Background & Context
The Challenge

Confronting Complexity in Financial SaaS

The previous homepage presented **70+ financial services** in a single, unstructured view. This layout caused immense cognitive fatigue, making it exceptionally difficult for users to find core features like payments or direct utility bills quickly.

The information architecture lacked a clear visual hierarchy, burying high-priority transaction entry points under less frequently accessed promotional and secondary content blocks.

The Solution

One App, Every Financial Need

We unified payments, transfers, and wealth services into a connected ecosystem supercharged with rewards, promotions, and gamified engagement to elevate money management.

User Voices

"I wasn't sure where to start. It took too long to find what I needed."

"There are too many options on the homepage. I just got distracted by other services."

"I couldn't find a way to add money to my wallet right away."

"The homepage looks so busy, and I didn't know what was important."

The Opportunity

How might we highlight essential services and reduce visual clutter?

Research
Competitive Benchmarking & Market Comparison

Market Comparison

Benchmarking Best-in-Class Platforms

We systematically analyzed major market leaders (Dolfin, Shopee Pay, Rabbit Line Pay) to find the perfect formula for clean card-based UI pathways, gamified user retention, and optimal service prioritization.

Key Insights

Streamlining Homepage Architecture

Unlike competitors that rely on endless scrolling and aggressive promotional banners, our redesign focuses heavily on immediate, click-accessible core transaction blocks.

User Engagement & Behavioral Insights
Behavioral Deep Dive

Understanding User Focus

Based on simulated support call logs and usage statistics, **100% of analyzed users** prioritize direct, rapid transaction workflows (checking balances, adding money, and scanning barcodes).

Conversely, advertisements and third-party promotional panels received the absolute least amount of focus (under 20% engagement). This data directly drove our layout simplification process.

Design Focuses
<epam>

Focus 1: Transactions

Balancing rapid transaction workflows with streamlined visual components on the primary wallet cards.

Redesigned UI

Wallet Balance Placement

Prioritized the wallet balance on the home screen, placing it alongside primary actions since checking available funds is users’ first task before scanning to pay at 7-Eleven or other merchants.

Decluttered Action Pathways

Moving less frequently used functions like “Transfer” and “Add Friend” into a secondary drop-down menu ensures a cleaner interface without cognitive overwhelm.

Outcome Goal

This structural arrangement minimizes active navigation time and seamlessly supports highly efficient core task completion.

Original UI

Focus 2: Gamified Coin Rewards System

Integrating gamified systems to trigger deeper engagement loops and repeated transactions.

Redesigned UI

Mission Relocation

Moved the “Complete Mission” card directly from the nested services sub-menu into the primary transaction area.

Unified Interaction Zone

By locating the coin rewards in the same area as transactions, users can seamlessly check their active rewards and make payments simultaneously.

Motivational Design

This immediate visibility acts as a positive feedback trigger, encouraging users to engage with missions and rewards more frequently.

Original UI

Focus 3: Bill Payments & Promos

Making high-traffic bill payment tiles and engaging promotional panels more visually attractive and direct.

Redesigned UI

Eye-Catching Card Layouts

I designed 'Deals' cards with highly engaging imagery and bold discount badges, instantly highlighting value to entice clicks.

Clear Value Proposition

Displaying discount pricing prominently captures visual focus, allowing users to rapidly find valuable deals without digging through menus.

Conversion Loop

This visual approach increases click-through rates and successfully motivates users to interact more with promotional offerings.

Original UI

Focus 4: Financial Prioritization

Decluttering the lower sections of the homepage by establishing clean categorical divisions.

Redesigned UI

Low-Priority Demotion

Arranged "Financial" and "Recommended financial services" as lower priority cards towards the bottom of the home screen layout.

Usability Boost

This organizational change dramatically minimizes visual noise, highlighting high-priority transaction tools where users expect them.

Engagement Cleanliness

Keeping secondary options cleanly separated ensures users find primary payment cards satisfying and highly accessible.
